After the data collection stage, in which we recorded the audio, we annotated the data. For this task, we used Label Studio, 
a platform for data labeling and evaluation. There, we were able to establish the ground truth by comparing the audio 
descriptions of onset and offset times with what was actually in the recordings.

A key feature we looked out for was **polyphony**: multiple sound events occurring simultaneously. 
This would play a part in model selection and training, since overlapping events make the task a multi-label problem.
